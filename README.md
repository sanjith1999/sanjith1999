## Hi, I'm Sanjith

I build the physical layer of communication and sensing systems — satellite and HF modems,
spread-spectrum waveforms, and the receivers that have to keep working at low SNR. Signal
processing carried the whole way down: from a model, through fixed point, into RTL on an
FPGA, and onto hardware that has to behave.

I believe research should be grounded in industry-driven challenges, because practical
innovation is what ultimately shapes the future.

**Currently** leading DSP/FPGA development on secure communication systems, and working on
delay-Doppler waveforms.

**Open to** a conversation about signal processing and hardware, and to contract work.

Some of my simpler work lives on my other account,
